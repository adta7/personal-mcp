Most of my time goes to the Protein Design toolkit and to writing up what measuring it
taught me. The pipeline itself produces educational-grade numbers by design — simplified
force fields, short trajectories, hosted models — but the questions it raised about *how
structure prediction gets scored* do not depend on that, and those are the ones worth
writing down carefully.
